The Words and Word Search game lexicon is derived from the International/3of6game.txt list in 12dicts 6.0.2, compiled by Alan Beale.

The 12dicts compiler explicitly released the lists to the public domain and requested acknowledgment. DoPop uses a filtered subset of unannotated ASCII words from 3 to 7 letters, then generates its own puzzle levels and thematic selections. This notice does not claim that the original 12dicts list was created by DoPop or relicense its source data.
